Terms of Use
================

.. warning::

   **DRAFT -- pending PI and legal review, not final.** The AVS dataset's public terms of
   use / data-sharing agreement have not yet been finalized. The summary below reflects the
   *direction* of internal draft notes made ahead of public release, paraphrased for this
   page -- it is **not** binding legal text and must not be relied upon or quoted as such.
   Do not treat anything on this page as a substitute for the final, published terms.

What's Expected to Be True
--------------------------------

Based on internal planning notes, the public release is expected to:

- Grant non-exclusive access for research and educational use.
- Require citation of the dataset paper (see :doc:`citation`) in any resulting publication.
- Prohibit redistribution of the raw or derivative data outside the terms of the agreement.
- Prohibit attempts to re-identify participants from anatomical MRI or other data.

All of the above is **subject to change** before the final terms are published.

What's Not Yet Decided
---------------------------

- The exact legal text of the data-use agreement.
- Whether access will require any form of registration or click-through acceptance, and how
  that interacts with mirroring the data elsewhere (the primary host is settled -- see
  :doc:`../data_access`).
- Whether any additional agreement (e.g. co-authorship expectations for early access) will
  apply outside of the final public release.
